## Long description (rich text)

**Get found when Indian shoppers ask AI.**

Search is turning into conversation. When a shopper asks *“best vitamin C serum
for Indian skin under ₹1,000”*, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answer with a
shortlist. AI Visibility helps your store earn a place on that shortlist — with
honest measurement, not snake-oil guarantees.

**What the app does**

- **AI Readiness Score** — audits crawlability, schema, content and brand
  signals across 30+ checks and gives you a 0–100 score with a fix-it list.
- **AI Visibility Tracker** — monitors your mention rate per query across
  ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Claude and Microsoft Copilot,
  with a daily snapshot and competitor comparison.
- **Schema Builder** — one-click Organization, Product and FAQ JSON-LD with
  ₹ prices, injected through your Shopify theme.
- **Smart Blogger** — generates Indian-English / Hinglish SEO articles from your
  real catalog and publishes them to your Shopify blog in one click.
- **AI Traffic → Orders** — sees which AI platform sent you paying customers via
  the orders/paid webhook and shows revenue per channel.
- **llms.txt, robots.txt & sitemap automation** for AI crawlers (App Proxy).

**Why brands in India choose it**

- Built for Indian D2C: INR billing, Hinglish support, WhatsApp-first help.
- Honest by design — we measure real signals and never promise a guaranteed
  "rank #1 in ChatGPT".
- Free plan included. No credit card to start.
